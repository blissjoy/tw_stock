# Workspace Instructions

## Taiwan Market Forecasts

When asked to forecast or review Taiwan stock-market or index direction, first read [`ai/MARKET_FORECAST_LOG.md`](ai/MARKET_FORECAST_LOG.md) and the relevant Zhu Jia-hong rules under `ai/zhu-rules/`. Follow the documented method, data-as-of requirements, and forecast logging rules. Append each original forecast before its outcome is known; later add only post-market verification without rewriting the forecast.
